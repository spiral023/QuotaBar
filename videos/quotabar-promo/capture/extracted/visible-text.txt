QuotaBar for Windows
Tagline: Know your AI spend. At a glance.
Real-time quota tracking, cost analytics & usage history for Claude & Codex.

Problem: you hit "Rate limit reached" mid-session in Claude Code / Codex, with no warning.

Features:
- Lives in your Windows tray: stacked per-provider progress bars (Claude orange, Codex indigo).
- Live quota progress: 5-hour and weekly windows, countdown to reset, burn rate (%/h), "On Track" pace badge, window budget forecast ("At current pace: will last until reset").
- Cost analytics & reports: API-equivalent USD cost (e.g. "API EQ. (30D) $594"), subscription ROI (e.g. "SUB ROI 21.2x"), daily/weekly/monthly cost charts, cache hit 97.3%, active days, avg session.
- Model insights: top models by cost (opus, sonnet, gpt, haiku), cost share %, cost vs intelligence scatter, token types.
- Smart alerts: Windows notifications before a quota limit and when windows reset ("Claude weekly window reset — 0% used").
- Permanent history: backfills daily, survives provider log cleanup.
- Privacy-first: reads only known local CLI paths (~/.claude, ~/.codex), redacts secrets. Offline-first, your data stays yours.
- Built for developers: Electron, TypeScript, open source (MIT). Free.
